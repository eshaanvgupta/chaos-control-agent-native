# Product overview

CHAOS//CONTROL helps retailers and inventory teams explore disruption response before committing operational decisions. The product turns supplied inventory, suppliers, order lines, warehouse capacities and routes into an abstract interactive twin. Computed delivery risk becomes a decision workflow: diagnose, calculate schedules, compare assumptions, approve one plan, execute the internal model, and verify the accounting.

Users can skip missions and import real operational tables. Sample data is labeled synthetic and creates no claims about actual customers, revenue or integrations. The core value works without a language model; the native agent adds contextual interpretation and tool orchestration when connected.

The distinguishing feature is isolated, reproducible counterfactual branches tied to explicit human approvals and shared agent/UI actions. This is decision support, not an ERP replacement. Plans do not guarantee real-world arrivals, model uncertainty is not a fabricated percentage, and execution is not a carrier or supplier transaction. There is no payment processing, claimed enterprise certification, customer traction or public multiplayer service.

Potential commercial development could add workspace collaboration, higher simulation volume and real commerce/warehouse integrations. Those are potential extensions, not advertised delivered capabilities.
