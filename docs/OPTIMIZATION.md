# Numerical model and limits

All costs are integer USD cents; quantities are nonnegative whole units. Every order row is one SKU order line with its own priority, zone and due-day offset. Day offsets are relative to the model's origin, not live calendar delivery promises.

The deterministic allocator enumerates direct warehouse fulfillment, one warehouse transfer followed by fulfillment, and supplier replenishment followed by fulfillment. It enumerates departure days within the horizon and scores candidates. Normal BASELINE replenishes from regular suppliers priced at no more than 600 cents per unit, without transfers or emergency suppliers. ECONOMY uses existing stock and optional interwarehouse transfers, avoiding purchases. BALANCED adds purchases and scores cost, lateness and arrival time. RESCUE heavily penalizes lateness and prefers earlier arrival. Priority weight changes the order sequence. These are documented heuristics, not proofs that the named objective reaches a mathematical optimum.

Each reservation debits initial warehouse stock or the SKU-specific supplier availability. Every lane has a per-departure-day reservation and each active warehouse has a per-processing-day reservation. Purchases cannot depart before the modeled supplier lead time. Arrival includes each lane's transit time. Blocked lanes and inactive warehouses cannot carry flow. A transfer requires both source and destination processing capacity. Total projected cost cannot exceed budget. Incoming volume must fit storage **without counting future outbound space**; this conservative constraint can reject physically possible schedules but cannot create stock or overfill storage.

Zero-day lanes are supported. Lane transit/handling assumptions are deterministic; no uncertainty distribution is sampled. Supplier reliability is a descriptive input and is never displayed as computed forecast confidence.

Allocation cost = quantity × (supplier purchase cost, when applicable + all route costs). Partial fulfillment is permitted; an order line counts fulfilled only when its full quantity is allocated. On-time service = 100 × fully on-time order lines / total order lines. Late units = fulfilled units arriving after deadline. Revenue exposure = sum of unallocated or late units × product sale cents. It is gross sale value exposure, not net loss or a revenue recognition statement.

Stockout count includes SKUs with open demand and zero aggregate current warehouse stock. Health = round(0.8 × unrounded on-time service percentage + 0.2 × 100 × (1 − stockout SKUs / total SKUs)). Mission rating uses on-time percentage: S ≥98, A ≥90, B ≥75, C ≥50, otherwise D. Zero Casualties requires every order on time. Cost Surgeon requires at least 90% service and modeled cost at most $2,500. Recovery Confirmed requires a service increase over the recorded baseline. These labels never alter objective scores or feasible allocations.

Applying a plan advances internal time by its horizon, debits consumed stock/supplier capacity and records modeled order fulfillment. It **does not confirm physical delivery**. Undo restores the preceding internal snapshot through a new audit event. Replay writes a historical snapshot into a new branch. Forecast assumptions may differ from the real world; no probabilistic confidence, exhaustive route search, multi-echelon replenishment, basket grouping, returns or global optimality is claimed.
